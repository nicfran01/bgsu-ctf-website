STORY
  It is Halloween night, and you have come home with a treat bag from seven
  houses on Elm Street. Every house left you a note, but every note is wearing
  a costume. Some of the houses were generous. Others played tricks. Take the
  costumes off, work out which houses you can trust, and rebuild the real
  flag.

WHAT YOU ARE GIVEN
  * trail.txt     the seven notes, one per house (read the top of it first)

WHAT YOU NEED
  * A text editor to read trail.txt
  * CyberChef (gchq.github.io/CyberChef) and/or Python 3 to take the
    costumes off. The "Spot it" tips from the Crypto Night Encoding examples
    slide will help you tell the costumes apart.

RULES
  * Flags look like BOO{...}. Submit the whole thing, braces included.
  * Not every BOO{...} you find is the real one. Read carefully.
  * Stuck? Ask an officer for a hint. We will give you one, not the answer.
